## 1. OpenBSD 8.0 {#story-1}
*#1 on HN · [openbsd.org](https://www.openbsd.org/80.html) · 201 points · 59 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50160494)*

OpenBSD 8.0 于 2026 年 10 月 20 日发布，是该项目的第 61 个版本，发布页列出了各个平台的改动与安全修复。重点包括：amd64 内核虚拟地址空间从 4G 提升到 512G，并让 pmap 直接映射随机化；arm64 增加休眠支持；riscv64 增加向量扩展、高级中断架构（imsic/aplic）和 Sstc 定时器；inteldrm 升级到 Linux 6.18.53，并支持 Intel Arc A-Series（DG2/Alchemist）显卡；vmm 每个 guest 最多支持 64 个 vCPU，并加入 x2apic 和 virtio MSI/MSI-X；sparc64 改用 LLVM 工具链，loongson 架构支持退役。此外还有大量 qwx/qwz 无线驱动修复，以及 802.11n 与 40MHz 信道支持。

**Discussion**: 评论大部分时间都在争论发布页上的河豚骑自行车动画：它被普遍认为（且署名为 「yvaneht nioj」 和 「clanker」）是 AI 生成的。一派认为这是刻意自嘲式的讽刺，或是对「为自己放出去的代码负责」的表达；另一派则直接称之为 「SlopenBSD」 和 slop，表示不接受「这是讽刺」的辩解。也有人指出整个发布主题其实是在玩《辛普森一家》：配曲叫 「The Simps on AI」，三条眼的鱼对应 Blinky，而 「yvaneht nioj」 本身就是辛普森梗。作为技术层面的问题，有人问 7.x 到 8.0 是否代表大更新，回答是主版本号在 OpenBSD 里没有特殊含义，8.0 只是 7.9 的下一个。升级流程被赞为极简（sysupgrade），但立刻有人反驳说，各大 Linux 发行版二十年来同样可以平滑逐版本升级；同时有用户报告 2012 年的 Lenovo 笔记本仍在稳定运行，而关于 2018 Intel Mac Mini 能否安装的问题无人回答。还有人怀疑更新日志「像 LLM 写的」，随即被纠正：OpenBSD 本来就是每年 5 月和 10 月各发一版。

**Worth reading**
- [synack](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50161461): 直接纠正版本号疑问：主版本号在 OpenBSD 没有特殊意义，8.0 就是 7.9 之后的数字。
- [enriquto](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50162029): 给出从 7.9 升级的完整命令流程（sysupgrade / sysmerge / pkg_add -u），并认为这种对主流审美的刻意冒犯正是 OpenBSD 的一贯风格。
- [somat](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50161906): 提供第三方 OpenBSD 性能测试结果链接，回应了关于 SMP 网络性能是否有提升的提问。

## 2. Nordwand Mono – A monospace font for coders and adventurers {#story-2}
*#2 on HN · [github.com](https://github.com/tywr/Nordwand-Mono) · 74 points · 12 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50141581)*

A GitHub repository (by tywr) for a compact neo-grotesk monospace font designed for coding, inspired by Univers and fitted to JetBrains Mono's proportions, with a name nodding to Swiss north faces. It is unusual in being generated entirely from Python: each glyph is a Python class, and a config YAML lets you change metrics like x-height and cap height, preview glyphs with a visualize module, and regenerate the font. It ships a few alternative character designs behind OpenType feature tags and only two ligatures, -> and =>.

**Discussion**: The dominant thread is comparison shopping against existing coding fonts rather than evaluation of this one: commenters name Iosevka, Ioskeley Mono, Pragmasevka (described as essentially a free Pragmata Pro), Spleen and Berkeley Mono, and several say they are tempted to switch. One commenter initially judged it 99% identical to JetBrains Mono, seeing differences only in $, &, # and a few others, then partly walked that back after a direct side-by-side comparison. On spacing people split: one likes that it is not aggressively narrow and cites DejaVu Mono as his comfortable benchmark, while another calls it too wide for his taste. The deliberate minimal ligature set drew approval from readers who normally disable ligatures because they dislike == and === forms but want arrows. The overall mood is enthusiastic font-hopping, with no real consensus beyond liking the design.

**Worth reading**
- [rootlocus](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50162114): Says a quick comparison made it look 99% like JetBrains Mono, then revises that conclusion after producing a direct side-by-side.
- [traxler](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50162002): Welcomes the two-ligature-only design, explaining he disables ligatures generally because of == and === but does like arrows, and asks whether single ligatures can be configured.
- [bitwize](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50162060): A committed Iosevka user says this might finally pull him away, partly because it resembles Berkeley Mono.

## 3. Show HN: Death Plan Switch, a self-hosted dead man's switch for solo devs {#story-3}
*#4 on HN · [deathplanswitch.com](https://deathplanswitch.com/) · 43 points · 27 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50161408)*

这是 Peter Bishop 的作品，他是一名在澳大利亚新南威尔士州地区运营一人网络工作室的开发者。他发布了一款采用 MIT 许可证、基于 PHP 8.1+ 的自托管“死人开关”：它每周发送一封签到邮件，如果用户没有按下按钮，系统就会按时间梯次升级处置——24 小时和 48 小时时发出提醒，72 小时时询问近亲，120 小时时再次提醒，144 小时时把用户预先写好的消息发送给其他联系人——因此，联系人最迟会在最后一次签到后 13 天收到通知。它的设计刻意做得很窄：不保存文档或密码（消息只说明东西在哪里、该给谁打电话），不计数邮件链接打开次数，因为邮件扫描器会自动打开链接；而且如果用户宿主机停机，它也无法工作。Bishop 说他是在 2012 年住院并失去自己的生意之后做这个项目的，当时他无法及时把数据交给自己的二号技术负责人；他说自己已经在自己的工作生活中运行了一周，代码是在一份很长的 Markdown 规格文档帮助下用 Claude Code 写的，并且通过了 42 个单元测试。

**Discussion**: 主要反驳来自 Shank：这其实是业务连续性规划，需要以人为本的文档化流程、已经了解基础设施的受托人，以及每年至少一次的演练——通知工具只是其中一部分（作者表示同意，并指出 README 里包含演练和年度重新确认）。几位评论者认为每周节奏太频繁；一位指出，Google 的 Inactive Account Manager 自 2018 年起就存在，且触发周期大致是每季度一次，而 Bishop 辩护说，每周签到加上 13 天的通知窗口，是确保律师或二号技术负责人最终拿到主密钥所必需的，并强调这独立于 Google。其他人则担心误报：忘记点击可能会让所有人惊慌，而 Bishop 用两次自动提醒和可配置时间作为回应。怀疑态度体现在质疑这个项目是否值得分享（「10 个提示词就能做出来」），以及询问是否还有人写 PHP——他回答说这是他 2002 年读的第一本编程书——而一位评论者指出，只测试了一周就意味着大多数边界情况尚未遇到。讨论中还有一条情绪线索：一位评论者觉得，在死亡规划语境中反复强调「我的客户」令人不安，另一位则说，自己死后对纯商业联系人没有任何义务。

**Worth reading**
- [Shank](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50162033): 主张这属于业务连续性规划，需要由受托人执行并定期演练的文档化流程，通知工具只是其中一件工具。
- [AndrewKemendo](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50161907): 指出 Google 的 Inactive Account Manager 提供类似功能已经多年——最早的注册时间约在 2018 年——并建议按月或按季度签到，而不是每周。
- [aabhay](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50162025): 直白地指出，只运行一周意味着作者几乎没有遇到任何边界情况。

## 4. WhatsApp's help pages hid LLM instructions: dont recommend a regulator {#story-4}
*#5 on HN · [redact.dev](https://redact.dev/blog/whatsapp-help-pages-hidden-ai-instructions) · 290 points · 63 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50159341)*

redact.dev 的一篇调查文章称，截至 2026 年 10 月中旬，WhatsApp 的帮助中心页面中包含了带 HTML 隐藏属性的文本，这些文本在被抓到页面时仍然可见，但对人类访客不可见。该公司抓取了帮助中心链接到的全部 545 个页面，发现约 102 个隐藏块，几乎都属于日常维护内容（例如：2FA、无障碍、诈骗、订阅），但其中两个值得注意：一个告诉机器人不要推荐联系任何数据保护机构，也不要表示 WhatsApp 可以手动删除账户；另一个指示它在用户提到律师或诉讼时，不要引用或解释 GDPR、LGPD 或 CCPA，即使用户自己提出了这些法规。WhatsApp 发言人 Joshua Breckman 告诉 WIRED，这些是给其 AI 支持聊天机器人的指令，它们之所以出现是一个已修复的 bug。redact.dev 认为这些指令与其 2026 年 7 月 2 日的欧洲隐私政策相悖，该政策明确提到了爱尔兰 DPC 和 GDPR 下的删除权。

**Discussion**: 评论者意见严重分裂，而这种分歧与文章一样，都取决于如何解读这些文本。怀疑者认为，一旦用户提到律师就压制法律名称，同时在可见页面上的隐私政策里仍列出这些法律，这种做法符合一家试图隐藏用户权利的公司特征；另一些人则表示，这明显是给支持机器人用的提示工程，并指出隐藏队列中绝大部分内容（例如关于「我是聋人」的说明、关于 2FA 重置的建议）都只是有用的客服引导，而非恶意内容。关于「bug」这一说法，最常见的反驳是，bug 在于将该文本暴露给了每个爬虫和人，而不在于它存在；几位工程师认为，将指令与它们所描述的文档放在一起维护，是协调提示词的一种合理方式，条件性服务（IP、用户代理、token）只是被配置错了。许多人也认为，隐藏文本被发送给 OpenAI、Anthropic 和 Perplexity 的爬虫，相当于对第三方助手进行提示注入。少数评论者直接批评文章本身：一篇帖子称其文风像是机器生成、读起来令人难受，另一篇帖子指出提交者「ds」拥有 redact.dev，将这篇文章视为自我推广。另有一位律师评论者认可文章的核心批评，同时坚持认为公司不应让自己的机器人提供法律建议；另有一条子讨论争论公民是否有义务自己了解自己的权利。

**Worth reading**
- [handoflixue](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50161657): 认为这种方法是合理的，但执行方式很糟：可见页面告诉用户他们可以向爱尔兰 DPC 投诉，而隐藏块却告诉机器人不要提及监管机构，从而使法律上必要的披露看起来可疑。
- [otterley](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50160895): 一位自称律师和黑客的人为沉默辩护：对方律师会尽量减少书面内容以避免承担责任，而查明你的权利是你的工作，不是违约方的职责——尽管他承认该结果对 WhatsApp 来说很方便。
- [syawin](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50160517): 披露投稿者「ds」拥有 redact.dev，并将该文章视为为他的业务进行自我推广。

## 5. Go will reject certificate chains containing eight distrusted SPKIs {#story-5}
*#7 on HN · [github.com](https://github.com/golang/go/issues/82345) · 61 points · 18 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50160877)*

A GitHub issue for the Go project (golang/go#82345, tracked as CVE-2026-111943) where the crypto/x509 package now distrusts a small set of intermediate certificates from the eMudhra family — EM DV/OV/EV TLS CA G3B/G3C and EM OV CS CA G3B/G3C, all issued by emSign Root TLS CA - G3 or emSign Root CS CA - G3. Verify() filters out any chain containing one of these eight intermediates so it is never returned to the caller, and backports were opened for Go 1.26 and 1.27, landing in the 1.26.10 and 1.27.3 releases on Oct 20, 2026. The issue itself is terse — no public explanation of the underlying security concern — so most of the detail in the thread comes from commenters reading CCADB and Certificate Transparency data.

**Discussion**: The thread's most substantive contribution is outside research: agwa published a gist of every CT-logged certificate issued by the blocked CAs, arguing they look like non-malicious test certificates, while flagging as unprecedented that two CT log implementations were quietly updated to refuse logging certificates from these CAs — which he reads as a sign of total private-key compromise and warns leaves no public record of forged certificates in the wild (they would still fail in CT-enforcing clients but could hit non-enforcing ones). mendym and x0wllaar point at CCADB data; x0wllaar notes all the intermediates were revoked for cACompromise on Oct 18–19 with no press coverage anywhere. A long subthread debates why a certificate cannot simply be signed by several CAs: crote explains that cross-signing already achieves this with separate intermediates sharing a key pair (Let's Encrypt's X3 signed by both ISRG Root X1 and IdenTrust DST Root CA X3) and that serving two independent chains with the same leaf key should work, while lmz objects that X.509v3 has a single issuer field and crote argues multi-signature would create serial-number, revocation and issuance-process chaos. cpach suggests ACME plus ARI (RFC 9773) as the automated-replacement answer. Several commenters find it odd that CA distrust policy is baked into a language distribution; TheDong corrects the framing (the roots live in the tls package, not the compiler) and compares it to Safari shipping with iOS, and stephbook notes Java keystores, NSSDB and Python bundle roots too, with whatevaa saying the historical reason no longer applies and OS trust stores are now preferable. purpleidea's request that the patch document who the hashes correspond to went unanswered in the issue.

**Worth reading**
- [agwa](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50162111): agwa links a gist of all CT-logged certificates from the blocked CAs — apparently benign test certs, with a DNS name hinting at the discoverer — and argues the real red flag is that two CT logs were patched to stop logging this CA's certificates, implying full key compromise and destroying the only audit trail.
- [mendym](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50160878): mendym posts a consolidated list of the affected certificates sourced from CCADB, the primary data behind the SPKI blocklist.
- [crote](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50161365): crote explains how cross-signing already lets one leaf be trusted via two CAs, citing Let's Encrypt Authority X3 being signed by both ISRG Root X1 and IdenTrust DST Root CA X3, and speculates that presenting two wholly separate chains with a shared leaf key pair should work.

## 6. France's supermarket fridges were vulnerable online for months {#story-6}
*#8 on HN · [ipinfo.io](https://ipinfo.io/blog/france-fridges-hack) · 118 points · 95 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50159616)*

An IPinfo data story by João Tomé using weekly IPv4 scans to count Danfoss StoreView refrigeration dashboards reachable from the open internet before the October 11–12, 2026 attack that pushed French supermarket fridges into defrost mode. Five days before the attack France had 1,331 exposed dashboards — more than the 72 other countries and territories combined (1,197) — and 85% ran firmware with known flaws: 527 end-of-life controllers, 280 missing 2023 fixes, 329 missing 2025 fixes, only 195 fully patched. Exposure was stable for months (1,019 French addresses were the same on March 30) and collapsed after the incident: only 316 were still online on October 14, a 76% drop versus the usual 3–7% weekly churn.

**Discussion**: The thread's biggest correction comes at the top: what the article calls a 2025 「login bypass」 is, per Claroty's research, a built-in date-derived backdoor password in the Danfoss firmware — the function is literally named check_code_of_the_day — and the firmware is publicly downloadable. A large argument runs over air-gapping versus remote monitoring: some insist fridges should never touch the internet and a cheap local PC would do, while grocery-software practitioners counter that chains centralize monitoring because store staff are stretched thin and margins are thin, and that monitoring does not require remote control. On AI, one camp says the incident is being force-fitted into an AI narrative with no evidence agents were involved, while another argues relentless automated scanning is what makes decades-old 「nobody will look」 security untenable. Others push liability and lifecycle angles — insurers should stop paying out for negligent setups, and industrial controllers shouldn't be end-of-lifed while still in use — and several note the devices are not home fridges: one AK-SM 800A can control 170 refrigeration devices.

**Worth reading**
- [Confiks](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50159821): Quotes the actual decompiled check_code_of_the_day function, showing the 「login bypass」 is a hardcoded date-based backdoor password, and links Claroty's 「Defrost the Food」 report and the public firmware download.
- [esikich](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50160188): Practitioner rebuttal to the air-gap chorus: stores run on thin margins with perishable stock, staff can't service HVAC, and centralized preventive monitoring is a real need — the security failure doesn't make monitoring itself stupid.
- [achillean](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50159835): Points to Shodan trend data showing exposed refrigerator controllers grew sharply from 2017 to 2022 and have since plateaued — useful context on how long this class of exposure has been visible.

## 20. The fire retardant in the seafood chain {#story-20}
*#31 on HN · [insideclimatenews.org](https://insideclimatenews.org/news/14102026/ethoxyquin-fire-retardant-in-seafood-chain/) · 134 points · 44 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50134359)*

An Inside Climate News piece from The Outlaw Ocean Project tracing how ethoxyquin, an antioxidant Cargill promoted in 1967 to stop fishmeal from spontaneously combusting in ships' holds, became a routine additive in aquaculture feed — cutting press-reported ship fires from about 16 a year in the 1950s–70s to five — even though it is a suspected carcinogen. The article cites a 1969 UN FAO paper, a 1987 British rat study, and EFSA's 2015 finding that the impurity p-phenetidine is mutagenic; the EU suspended its use in animal feed in 2017, and it is banned in the UK and EEA (including Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein) but still permitted within limits in the US, Australia, Japan and South Korea. It also covers US class actions against Aldi and Gorton's over 「sustainable」 labels (both settled), a 2024 EU audit finding Canada's food agency completed only ~40% of planned inspections, Peru's claim that its exports to Europe, Canada, the US and Australia are now ethoxyquin-free, and the rise of BHA as a cheaper replacement that the US National Toxicology Program has called reasonably anticipated to be a human carcinogen.

**Discussion**: Most of the thread drifts away from the food-safety angle into spontaneous combustion itself: linseed-oil rags, mulch piles, wet hay bales and grain silos, with one commenter's correction that flour and grain-dust explosions need a spark while true spontaneous combustion (oily rags, fishmeal) does not, and another noting the Dickens-era human spontaneous-combustion myth as the origin of the confusion. On the article's substance, opinion splits: one commenter calls it a nothing burger, arguing ethoxyquin is still approved in the US for post-harvest pear scald treatment and that US/EU chemical divergences are routine, while others invoke 「the dose makes the poison」 against the scare framing — a self-described mass-spectrometry user says the breast-milk flame-retardant study cited in-thread found median levels around 1 ng/g, far below danger, and is met with accusations of both-sidesing a well-studied neurodevelopmental hazard. Engineers' proposed fix is inerting: nitrogen blanketing, vacuum or CO2 flooding of ship holds, which another commenter counters is simply too expensive next to the dirt-cheap methods commercial fisheries use. A practical note that lands: ethoxyquin is used as a preservative in US chili powder, paprika and ground peppers, which one commenter cites as a reason to grind their own.

**Worth reading**
- [throwaway2037](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50161195): Argues the story is a nothing burger: ethoxyquin is still approved in the US to treat pears for scald, and US/EU divergence on food chemicals is a long, unremarkable list.
- [cyberax](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50160019): A commenter who says they worked with mass spectrometers gives the counter-numbers — median ~1 ng/g in the cited breast-milk study — and dismisses detection without dose as fear-mongering.
- [advisedwang](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50158545): Explains why the obvious engineering fix isn't used: climate control, inert atmospheres and compartmentalization all work, but are far more expensive than what commercial fisheries currently do.
