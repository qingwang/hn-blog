## 1. Quad9 (DNS Resolver) to Exit France {#story-1}
*#1 on HN · [quad9.net](https://quad9.net/news/blog/au-revoir-france/) · 521 points · 185 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50195959)*

Quad9, the Swiss nonprofit that runs the free 9.9.9.9 privacy/security resolver, says a French court ruled against it in a case brought by beIN Sports — a Qatari-owned broadcaster with French offices — which demanded it block resolution of domains allegedly used to pirate sports broadcasts. The court cut the fine from €580,000 to €1,000 per day, but Quad9 says it can't comply: it keeps no user records, so it cannot scope blocking to France, refuses global blocking as extraterritorial censorship, and cites a German precedent where Sony bypassed its geoIP block via VPN and Quad9 was fined for contempt. It will withdraw service to French users on November 24, 2026, with roughly 20 more blocking lawsuits pending against it in France.

**Discussion**: The thread is split between outrage at the ruling, a long-running argument about whether copyright is rent-seeking by publishers rather than protection for authors, and a cluster of technically sharp objections to Quad9's own framing. Several commenters — including one who appears to speak for Quad9 — point out that the German blocking order that Quad9 was fined over was ultimately overturned by a higher German court in December 2023, which complicates the article's narrative of inevitable defeat. Others attack the stated impossibility of geoIP blocking as self-contradictory: if Quad9 cannot identify French users well enough to block specific domains, how does it plan to deny service to France entirely, since dropping French servers alone doesn't do that. A recurring correction is that the plaintiff is beIN Sports, not Sony — many commenters arrived angry at Sony and had to be redirected. There is also a practical thread for French users about alternatives (DNSCrypt, RethinkDNS, self-hosted Unbound over DoH/DoT), and a note that Cisco's OpenDNS already exited France and Portugal after similar injunctions, so consolidation onto fewer resolvers makes each new blocking order more effective. Rough consensus: the ruling is technically incoherent and unenforceable, but the courts can still bankrupt a small nonprofit, which is the point.

**Worth reading**
- [skrause](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50198272): Corrects the article's framing of the German case: the blocking order that Quad9 was fined for violating was ultimately overturned by a higher German court in December 2023, so Quad9 did win.
- [jiveturkey](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50198060): Flags an apparent inconsistency: Quad9 calls per-country geoIP blocking technically impossible, yet plans to deny service to France entirely — which requires the same IP-based geolocation.
- [euio757](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50197803): Adds precedent with links: Canal+ obtained similar injunctions against Google, Cloudflare and Cisco, and Cisco pulled OpenDNS out of France and Portugal entirely two years ago.

## 2. eMudhra to exit global SSL/TLS certificate business {#story-2}
*#2 on HN · [emudhra.com](https://emudhra.com/en/press-release/emudhra-to-exit-global-ssl-tls-certificate-business) · 697 points · 500 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50195110)*

A press release from eMudhra Limited (BSE: 543533), an Indian digital trust company, announcing that its board has decided to voluntarily withdraw from the global SSL/TLS certificate business after the incident where its CA keys turned out to be trivially weak. Chairman V. Srinivasan says SSL/TLS is only about 1% of global revenue, that the affected CAs were revoked within 14 hours of discovery, that no end-entity certificates were issued from them, and that no infrastructure or data was breached — while complaining that social media scrutiny was “out of proportion” to TLS’s share of the business. The company says its G1 RSA roots will keep serving customers through the transition, replacements will be free, and it will still deliver a full root-cause report to browser root programs; its DSC, eSign, emCA/CertiNext, emSigner, SecurePass and emAS products are claimed unaffected.

**Discussion**: The thread is almost uniformly hostile: commenters read the release as “you can’t fire me, I quit,” arguing the business was already dead because no browser or OS would ever trust eMudhra roots again, and that exiting does not release them from root-program obligations or investigation. Several pick apart the wording — “no end entity certificates issued” is close to meaningless when anyone could mint certificates from the compromised CA, though others note Certificate Transparency logging means no forged cert was actually used against a mainstream browser. First-hand reports dominate the practical side: some iPhones and Macs were still accepting the bad chains days later while Chrome revoked in about 1 hour 40 minutes and Firefox in under 4 hours, with battery-saver mode and stale trust stores blamed, and Apple's revocation confirmed in press coverage. A minority zooms out to argue the real failure is Web PKI itself — trusting hundreds of commercial CAs in many jurisdictions — and revives calls for DANE/DNSSEC and mandatory pinning, countered by the point that nobody proposing CA abolition offers a workable replacement. A large, uglier subthread generalizes from the incident to Indian management culture, regulators and cheap outsourcing, drawing pushback from those who say the approval-and-monitoring failure could have happened anywhere.

**Worth reading**
- [Arbortheus](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50197321): A user reports that nearly five days after disclosure their up-to-date iPhone 15 Pro still trusted certificates from three eMudhra intermediates, contrasting Chrome’s ~100-minute revocation and Mozilla’s ~4-hour one, and links a live test page and blog timeline.
- [BoppreH](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50196693): Line-by-line rebuttal of the press release: the CA private key is infrastructure data and was breached, no FIPS 140-2 Level 3 HSM would generate “4”, and the whole root is being revoked next week — which the release omits.
- [ieie3366](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50195217): Links the Chrome team’s aggressive question battery to eMudhra on Mozilla’s Bugzilla (bug 2083449), which other commenters compare to a cross-examination.

## 3. Nvidia OpenShell {#story-3}
*#3 on HN · [github.com](https://github.com/NVIDIA/OpenShell) · 66 points · 32 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50198675)*

OpenShell is Nvidia's open-source (Apache 2.0) runtime for running fleets of autonomous AI agents inside sandboxes. It enforces policies at the kernel level — restricting file access, system calls and network connections per agent — injects real credentials only into requests bound for approved endpoints, and uses formal verification to flag what a policy change would newly allow before it is applied. The 0.1.x line adds a stable release cadence and expanded extension APIs; it requires Linux, Apple Silicon macOS or WSL 2, plus Docker, Podman or host virtualization, ships SDKs for Node, Go and Rust, and collects anonymous telemetry by default (opt-out via OPENSHELL_TELEMETRY_ENABLED=false).

**Discussion**: The thread splits between interest in the concept and irritation at the defaults. The loudest complaint is telemetry being on by default, called 「insane」 and 「spyware」, with one commenter proposing to have a local model strip it out; the article's own install/quickstart material drew jokes about handing Claude root, or AWS credentials, so it can set up a tool meant to prevent exactly that. A long side argument corrects the 「video game card manufacturer」 framing of Nvidia — one commenter cites consumer gaming at about 5.5% of revenue and The Economist's 「Central Bank of AI」 label, others note gaming is now folded into an edge-computing reporting category — while a dissenter argues the control granularity is 「almost too pedantic」 and only fits narrow enterprise requirements, claiming the agentic world is moving away from Kubernetes. Two substantive questions went essentially unanswered: how OpenShell compares with other agent sandboxes and what distinguishes it, and where the industry is supposedly moving instead of k8s.

**Worth reading**
- [nickv](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50199412): Puts a number on the Nvidia identity debate: about 5.5% of revenue comes from consumer gaming cards, and links The Economist's 「Central Bank of AI」 briefing.
- [dangoodmanUT](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50199137): Argues default-on telemetry is unacceptable and that this level of per-call policy control is overkill for most companies outside nuanced enterprise requirements.
- [niemandhier](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50198676): First-hand report from GTC Europe that a significant share of demos were already using OpenShell, which the commenter had never heard of beforehand.

## 4. Show HN: Run full iOS 16.5 firmware on Android Devices {#story-4}
*#4 on HN · [github.com](https://github.com/oosexgang/hackios-xnu-kernel) · 162 points · 56 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50196718)*

OOSEX GANG 在 GitHub 上发布了一个仓库，它把 Apple 开源的 XNU 内核（xnu-8796.121.2，即 iOS 16.5 所用的内核）启动到 Xiaomi POCO X5 Pro 5G（代号 redwood，Snapdragon 778G）以及 QEMU 的 aarch64 virt 机器上，并在这之上运行未经修改的 iOS 16.5 用户态，依次经过 launchd、backboardd、SpringBoard 和应用。项目自带 EFI loader（即 xnuloader）、UEFI 移植、替代 Apple 闭源驱动的驱动程序，以及通过 LinuxKPI 兼容层运行的 ath11k Wi-Fi 驱动；仓库中不含任何 Apple 文件，ios165/fetch.sh 会下载 7.4 GB 的 IPA 并按需解包，在手机上则通过 fastboot boot 启动，不向闪存写入任何内容。它管自己叫研究试验台，而不是 ROM：一次会话持续固定时长，之后内核把日志交回 loader。它尚未实现的部分包括 App Store 内容和 Apple ID 登录（没有 Secure Enclave，FairPlay daemon 会退出）、蜂窝网络、蓝牙、充电（手机只会放电），以及完整的 Metal（456 个系统着色器中已有 293 个在 Adreno 上运行）。

**Discussion**: 评论者对这个里程碑感到震惊，尤其是相机在 Camera.app 中就能工作，因为在大多数设备上相机是 postmarketOS 的薄弱环节；但主要争论在于它究竟有没有用。最实质的反驳针对设备认证：一位评论者指出，iMessage、App Store 和普通应用都依赖设备认证，这正是把 Beeper Mini 搞垮的原因，而这张《地图》截图里，弹窗后面的地图瓦片是空白的，说明内置应用可能也失败了。对于整个这一类演示为何都止步于 iOS 16，有人给出了解释：iOS 17 引入了 Secure Page Table Monitor 和 Trusted Execution Monitor；另有几位则称赞了 iOS 16.5 这个内核版本，以及把手机当作一次性设备、不向闪存写入任何内容这一做法。有若干人开玩笑说这是「只能当一次性燃烧机用的手机」（内核下唯一能做的充电是放电），并怀疑 AI 时代的走红演示是否藏着恶意软件，而另一个人从项目网站上挖出了一个细节：它靠一种名为 $HACKIOS 的 BNB 链代币提供资金，每笔交易抽取 1% 的手续费。好几位评论者顺带回答了「为什么要把 iOS 放到 Android 上」这个问题，提到黑苹果（Hackintosh）的吸引力，而意料之中的“蓝气泡 iMessage”愿望则被反驳，说大概率无法实现，还有少数人希望出现反向版本，即 iOS 设备上运行 Android 或 GrapheneOS。

**Worth reading**
- [gruez](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50198119): 认为 App Store 的缺失才是关键：标准应用仍然会对后端接口强制执行设备认证，而地图弹窗后面的空白瓦片表明，内置应用已经失败；所以人们还不如用一套换上 iOS 皮肤的 Android。
- [LoganDark](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50199130): 认为演示之所以都止步于 iOS 16，是因为 iOS 17 增加了 Secure Page Table Monitor 和 Trusted Execution Monitor。
- [jrmg](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50199094): 指出项目的资金模式：一种名为 $HACKIOS 的代币，部署在 BNB 链上的 flap.sh，每笔交易抽取 1% 用于开发。

## 5. Getting ready to operate GNU Taler in Germany {#story-5}
*#5 on HN · [taler.net](https://www.taler.net/en/news/2026-13.html) · 93 points · 30 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50197404)*

NGI TALER 项目宣布在波鸿成立 Taler Operations AG，作为卢森堡 Taler Systems SA 的子公司，负责在德国运营面向欧元的 GNU Taler 兑换所（exchange），下一步将向德国联邦金融监管局 BaFin 申请电子货币机构（EMI）牌照。原计划由波鸿的 GLS 银行担任电子货币发行方，但 GLS 在 2026 年 1 月决定退出该角色（原因据称与 Taler 本身无关），新公司接手其职能，GLS 仍留在联盟中提供支持。在 BaFin 批准前无法开业，公司正以至少 5 万欧元起投的门槛向投资人募资；文章强调 Taler 协议和参考实现仍是自由软件，受监管的只是运营服务。

**Discussion**: 争论很快从公司架构转向 Taler 的隐私模型。最尖锐的批评来自 nullc，他主张 Taler 是「以复杂密码学掩盖的监控方案」：付款人匿名，但收款人的身份、金额都对兑换所可见，把它称作隐私保护是误导甚至欺诈；也有人反驳说这一取舍本来就公开写明了。多位评论者补充说，Taler 的隐私是单向的——买家匿名、商家可审计——这被支持者视为反洗钱和反逃税的设计，被批评者视为给政府留下完整资金流向。关于监管路径，traceroute66 推测它现在只是发行方而非支付处理商，因此兑换环节仍需 KYC，但之后的消费不会由 Taler 记录。另有一支讨论认为 GNU Taler 已错过时机，稳定币和区块链支付已抢占市场，随即有人反驳说即时低成本支付根本不需要区块链。技术侧有人指出其核心是 David Chaum 的盲签名电子现金，并确认 Chaum 本人参与其中。

**Worth reading**
- [nullc](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50198398): nullc 详细论证 Taler 并非真正隐私系统：收款方的身份和金额都会被附加到每笔支付上，称其为「隐私保护」是误导，甚至可能被用于敲诈、营销和诉讼等滥用。
- [traceroute66](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50198419): traceroute66 根据官网措辞推断新方案已从支付处理商退为纯发行方，因此兑换欧元成 Taler 时仍需 KYC，但之后的消费不在 Taler 记录范围内，并认为这在欧盟有先例。
- [rickydroll](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50198394): rickydroll 指出这本质上是 David Chaum 的 DigiCash/eCash 盲签名方案，并确认 Chaum 确实参与了项目，附上 chaum.com 的原始论文和 eCash 2.0 文档。

## 7. What Is Gridifcation {#story-7}
*#9 on HN · [musicalform.substack.com](https://musicalform.substack.com/p/what-is-gridifcation) · 299 points · 140 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50150163)*

An essay from the Musical Form Institute’s Substack comparing Billboard Hot 100 top fives from 1975 and 2025, using tempo analysis to argue that modern pop has been quantized into machine perfection. Its headline example: Bruno Mars and Lady Gaga’s 「Die with a Smile」 maintains 100% of its runtime within ±1 BPM, with zero tempo transitions, while Captain & Tennille’s 「Love Will Keep Us Together」 drifts and has kick and bass deliberately out of alignment. The author defines gridification as any post-performance temporal correction of a human performance — not playing to a click, not drum machines — grounds it in Susanne K. Langer’s aesthetics, and ends by promoting a paid 「Certificate of Embodied Production」 whose public beta launched that week.

**Discussion**: Most commenters accept the phenomenon is real and audible — a high school jazz band whose recording was snapped and autotuned into unrecognizability, a mashup producer noting Earth, Wind & Fire’s bar-to-bar drift, Rick Beato’s quantized-Bonham demo — but they split on whether it is new or bad. Several push back on the framing: click tracks and edit-room perfectionism date to the 1970s (Steely Dan’s Donald Fagen and the Wendel samplers, Lee Sklar’s no-click session story), and quantization makes weak players listenable rather than only ruining great ones. The sharpest objection is that the 2025 top five is largely hip-hop and vocal-forward electronica built on programmed drums, so measuring tempo variance measures the wrong thing — a defense the author partly pre-empts by excluding drum machines from the definition. A separate cluster suspects the Substack itself is LLM-written marketing for the certification, citing stock essay phrasing and articles that flag as AI-generated. Side threads recommend jazz, the Lemon Twigs, Burial’s Sound Forge workflow, and live music as antidotes.

**Worth reading**
- [jrickert](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50196895): A musician and producer reports that Suno-generated tracks show the same subtle tempo drift the article celebrates as human, and that he spends hours quantizing them in Ableton — making imperfection a possible AI tell.
- [diskzero](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50197706): Someone who worked in music hardware notes that Donald Fagen already did take-after-take surgery with Wendel samplers and Doctor Click controllers decades pre-DAW, so the technique predates the tools and the debate is really about taste.
- [jrajav](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50194734): Argues the essay’s premise assumes live drumming: Kendrick Lamar and Billie Eilish are machine-beat genres, hip-hop redefined groove by deliberately nudging beats off-grid, and tempo standard deviation alone papers over that.
