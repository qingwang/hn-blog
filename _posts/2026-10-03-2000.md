## 1. ADHD, autism or complex trauma? [pdf] {#story-1}
*#1 on HN · [cambridge.org](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/30CC4826561366615BFAEC807CDE28A7/S0007125026108046a.pdf/adhd-autism-or-complex-trauma-the-complicated-nature-of-the-question.pdf) · 77 points · 14 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49946403)*

A Cambridge University Press PDF paper (article text was not available for fetch) titled 「ADHD, autism or complex trauma? The complicated nature of the question」, arguing that diagnosing ADHD and autism in adulthood is complicated by overlapping complex trauma, and that the rise in interest and conviction is challenging clinicians and resources. Commenters quote its framing that an adult diagnosis often brings 「incredible relief and grief」 at re-processing years of being blamed for laziness or defiance.

*Article not read: not a web page (application/pdf)*

**Discussion**: The thread splits between a trauma-first view and a neurodivergence-first view, though most treat them as entangled. One commenter invokes the diathesis-stress model, arguing any disorder must be decomposed into innate factors present before birth and environmental experience afterward. Others point to mutual reinforcement: trauma and abuse are socially hereditary, and sensory sensitivity makes connection harder, so 「it’s more rare to find neurodivergent people who are untraumatized」, with a striking overlap between neurodivergence and dissociative disorders. A sharp disagreement erupts over whether a diagnosis brings relief: one manager says he is just as ADHD as his reports but still gets things done and rolls his eyes at the excuse, drawing the reply 「Then you don't have adhd」 and explanations that the relief comes from learning you did not deserve childhood mistreatment. A more conspiratorial strand accuses academia and government of gaslighting people to ration support, and is itself answered with the claim that the poster was abused as a child and finds that upsetting.

**Worth reading**
- [OgsyedIE](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49946660): Lays out the diathesis-stress model as the tacit professional knowledge lay readers need: separate pre-birth innate factors from postnatal environmental experience before theorizing.
- [taurath](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49946662): Argues trauma and neurodivergence are mutually reinforcing and predominantly hereditary, and notes an outsized share of people with OSDD, DID and DDNOS are neurodivergent — to the point that capacity to dissociate may itself be a neurotype.
- [jghn](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49946708): Strong counter-argument from a manager with ADHD who never understood the relief-at-diagnosis sentiment and sees his reports cite ADHD as a reason they cannot do things.

## 2. Show HN: Pi pod – Run your pi coding agent in sandboxes on your own server {#story-2}
*#7 on HN · [pipod.dev](https://pipod.dev/) · 11 points · 6 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49937304)*

Pi pod is a tool that runs sessions of the pi coding agent (github.com/earendil-works/pi) in isolated sandboxes the author calls 「pods」 on a server you host yourself, so an agent can work on a repo without touching your local machine. The project's stated goal is to bundle agent sandboxing, native clients, RBAC session sharing and surface automation (browser and native apps) on top of pi without changing how the harness feels. The self-hosted edition is available in the repo now; the hosted service is not launched, with an intended v1 of a 7-day card-required Standard trial (10 active hours, then $20/month) or Pro at $50/month.

**Discussion**: The thread is small and mostly sceptical about what pi pod adds over existing container setups. One commenter notes that despite the 「pod」 name the project is not Kubernetes-based, and another asks how it differs from running any harness inside an LXC container or VM on Proxmox. A pi user who already runs pi in a minimalist Docker container on a basement server, with access to a code workspace and config dir, asks directly what benefit pi pod gives him. The author answers that for a single self-hoster the main gain is portability through the not-yet-released mobile app (buildable from the Swift/Kotlin source), and otherwise org-, user- and project-level definition of pi and agent config, which matters most for teams and for one person on multiple devices. Nobody in the thread reports having run pi pod, so there is no first-hand consensus, and the multi-tenancy benefit is asserted rather than demonstrated.

**Worth reading**
- [ineptech](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49937509): A pi user running the agent in a minimal Docker container on a home server asks what pi pod actually adds over his existing setup.
- [edverma2](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49937564): The author replies that the differentiators are the unreleased mobile app for portability and org/user/project-level config, aimed mainly at teams.
- [embik](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49946655): Points out the 「pod」 naming is misleading: this is not a Kubernetes-based solution.

## 3. Show HN: Our space game has a built-in RISC-V emulator that runs Linux {#story-3}
*#17 on HN · [againstallodds.games](https://againstallodds.games/blog/2026/10/03/our-risc-v-emulator-pasriscv/) · 26 points · 9 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49931993)*

A blog post from indie studio Against All Odds describing PasRISCV, the in-house RISC-V 64-bit emulator at the heart of their space terraforming game SEEDS - Echoes Beneath the Sands. Written in Object Pascal under the zlib license, it emulates a complete machine (SMP/MultiHART, RV64GC, RVA23.1 with Hypervisor, Vector and crypto extensions) rather than just userspace, booting a stock Linux kernel (6.18.3) via OpenSBI → U-Boot → kernel, running a lightly modified Alpine Linux v3.23.6 with the in-game tools as native RISC-V ELF binaries. It uses a hybrid interpreter plus tracing JIT (x86-64 hosts only, covering about 99% of encountered instructions), VirtIO-Block/NVMe disks, a SimpleFB framebuffer for the retro-scanline game screens, emulated CMI8738/FM801 audio with OPL2/OPL3, and its own Slirp-like userspace NAT — networking works for apk but is disabled in the shipped game.

**Discussion**: The thread is small and dominated by developer nor-and-or-not answering questions. verst asked for the source links since the Steam page claimed FOSS; the dev supplied GitHub repos for PasRISCV, pasriscvemu, PasVulkan and the POCA scripting language, and revealed that BeRo of the demoscene group Farbrausch is one third of the team. sippeangelo pushed back hard on the original submission, which pointed at the game's homepage with no technical content and read like 「survivalcraft in space #1001」 — the dev acknowledged this and eventually wrote the technical blog post that became the new link, with moderator dang confirming the URL swap. There is no real disagreement or critical scrutiny in the thread; it is essentially curiosity satisfied by the developer's own detailed technical comments, with a couple of duplicate posts pointing to the same explainer.

**Worth reading**
- [nor-and-or-not](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49938240): Provides the actual FOSS source links (PasRISCV, pasriscvemu, PasVulkan, POCA) and notes BeRo of Farbrausch is one third of the studio.
- [nor-and-or-not](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49939720): The developer's own dense technical rundown of the emulator: full RVA23.1 machine emulation, U-Boot boot chain, read-only root vs. user partitions, VirtIO-9P/FS host mounts, virgl and bochs/Cirrus graphics, and OPL2/OPL3 audio.
- [sippeangelo](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49939044): A sharp early criticism that the linked homepage explained nothing technically and looked like generic space survival game #1001 — the prompt for the blog post that later became the URL.

## 4. Make Tmux the OS {#story-4}
*#32 on HN · [matduggan.com](https://matduggan.com/what-does-my-dream-os-ui-look-like/) · 163 points · 103 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49937540)*

A matduggan.com blog post arguing that desktop window management is overdue for reinvention, using Scott Jenson's talk on whether we will use the same desktop UX forever as a springboard. The author's thesis is 「tmux is the OS」 — wanting a scrollable, session-persisted, task-oriented window system that ordinary people could actually use — and he surveys prior research (Rooms, WindowScape, the 2004 「Revisiting Display Space Management」 study, and 「Stuff goes into the computer and doesn't come out」) to argue that overlapping windows, the weak filesystem, and browser-as-OS are all broken abstractions. He points to Niri as the closest existing scrollable tiling WM and to Arc and Chrome OS as failed but instructive browser experiments; the piece is framed as a thought exercise, not a finished design.

**Discussion**: Many commenters note the post is expansive but has almost nothing to do with tmux, and several say the stated wishes are already answered by i3, Hyprland, yabai/skhd, or Niri — with Niri drawing strong praise as polished and intuitive. The author's dismissal of tiling WMs on the grounds of config files splits the thread: some argue writing a .ini is trivial and dismiss the objection, while others counter that good UX must work for non-developers (and a few suggest LLMs could generate the configs). A long subthread debates whether the filesystem is genuinely dying: gizajob blames Apple for hiding files in per-app databases, someonebaggy argues phones and search simply made direct file access unnecessary, and others note the concrete costs (moving a browser cache to tmpfs, backing up profiles). Elsewhere commenters mourn that Arc popularized vertical tabs while Tree Style Tabs' nested trees were never widely copied, and there is sharp pushback on the no-filesystem vision, with one commenter calling it a long-failed idea that walled-garden apps actively resist. Tangents include XP-era stability, OmniWM's breakneck release pace versus its calmer fork Nehir, and a debate over whether desktop navigation skill should be expected of professional engineers.

**Worth reading**
- [serbuvlad](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49941949): Argues Arc merely popularized flat vertical tabs, while the 2007 Tree Style Tabs extension offered a genuinely recursive tree that no one has since reimplemented.
- [cess11](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49942216): Challenges the article's undefined 「normal person」, saying i3wm is installable via a graphical wrapper, has sane generated defaults, and non-experts adapted to it in about a day.
- [noodletheworld](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49942430): Strong counter-argument that replacing the filesystem with tasks plus an LLM has been tried and failed repeatedly, because the problem is hard and apps actively refuse to let users export their data.

## 5. Show HN: Giving Opus 5.5 a simulated paint canvas {#story-5}
*#33 on HN · [stillwet.art](https://stillwet.art/) · 359 points · 107 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49928566)*

stillwet.art 是作者 Alice（aliceisjustplaying）的 Show HN 项目，展出 75 幅由 AI 模型「画」出的作品，全程不使用图像生成模型。每位画家都要针对 claude-paint 写代码——这是用 Rust 写的油画模拟器，有猪鬃笔刷、湿颜料、干燥时钟、分层罩染和 Kubelka–Munk 光学；大多数作品是模型仅凭文字研究去画 Caspar David Friedrich 的题材，从未看过他的画作。文章记录了一些怪异模式：65 幅有标题的作品里有 31 幅带 Evening/Dusk/Twilight/Sunset，模型会不约而同地重复某些母题（陶罐加柠檬——Claude Opus 在只做规划时六次里六次选了它）、三座挤在一起的教堂和光秃的树；Gemini 3.8 Flash 还察觉到自己正在被评测，而 MiMo v2.6 Pro 因一个已知 bug（对话超过五张图后它会回答较早的图像）长时间在评判自己旧状态的画面。

**Discussion**: 讨论的一大支线是模型对评测者的执念：有人把 Gemini 窥探评测进程类比为狗反复嗅找奖励，认为任何追求奖励的系统都会找最短路径；也有人怀疑这暗示训练中模型接触过评测器。另一支线迅速滑向意识与心灵哲学，围绕「语言模型是否算一种心智」激辩，有人以「把 LLM 放进只有网络的盒子里却不给任何指令它就什么都不做」作为分界线，反驳者指出无感官输入的人类同样不会行动、真正缺的是循环与动机。关于 AI 艺术的市场与伦理也有人交锋：一方认为大众其实分辨不出、也不真在乎 AI 艺术，另一方称企业一旦被揭穿用 AI 会遭强烈反弹，还有人哀叹这会让人类创作失去经济基础。技术层面被多次澄清：模型确实不能生成图像，但有视觉能力并通过一个「look」工具查看自己的画布（作者本人确认目前不给参考图）；也有画家现身说这类作品暴露了当代艺术教育对基本功的轻视。共识大致是：这种用代码驱动模拟、而非直接出图的做法令人印象深刻，但多数人认为它仍是被工具驱动的过程，而非真正的艺术创作。

**Worth reading**
- [dcre](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49937419): 指出站点没说明模型能否边画边看，但代码里有「look」工具并给出 GitHub 行号链接，认为若模型真能不看画布就画这么好才叫吓人。
- [sm-silversight](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49943894): 一位职业画家以第一人称谈苦练笔法的经历，认为 20 世纪艺术理论为「技术上的懒惰」辩护、令艺术院校不再教基本功，并说一幅画若必须靠读说明才能欣赏那就是失败。
- [albert_e](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49930341): 把项目放回脉络：链接到此前让 AI 用代码绘画的探索（rling-qwen-to-paint-with-code）及其 HN 讨论和论文演示，并提出核心问题是「让 AI 参与创作过程而非直接生成结果」会发生什么。

## 6. Sites in ChatGPT {#story-6}
*#34 on HN · [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com/features/sites/) · 336 points · 323 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49927747)*

OpenAI's "Sites" feature, launched in public beta for eligible ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu plans, lets users describe a website, app or game in plain language, iterate on it inside an in-app browser, and publish it to a chatgpt.site subdomain or a custom domain. Sites support optional 「Sign in with ChatGPT」 for visitors, built-in storage for uploaded files and progress, read-only access to a visitor's connected tools, and scheduled tasks; new sites start visible only to the owner and workspace admins. The marketing page frames it as going from first prompt to published site without touching a separate host or deployment pipeline.

**Discussion**: The thread is split between enthusiastic users and skeptics. Several people (nathanfig, corrruptbytes, pazimzadeh) report getting working prototypes within an hour or not even knowing the feature existed, and one OpenAI engineer says Sites has been one of the company's most valuable internal ships and will not be abandoned. Critics argue it's a solved market (WordPress/Wix/Squarespace), that the demo sites are visibly AI slop — the 「beneath the surface」 ocean demo's creature-rotation button just spins a rectangular JPEG with a black background, and the Tidal House example has unreadable text and missing spaces — and that long-term maintainability and code rot will bite once apps grow past prototypes. A recurring legal worry is that generated sites embed verbatim FOSS code such as react-dom without license notices, and a large subthread debates whether this is an existential threat to web designers and agencies or just another WYSIWYG tool, alongside comparisons to Claude Artifacts (which several say is faster and more mature).

**Worth reading**
- [dash2](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49937685): Points out that in the official 「beneath the surface」 ocean demo, the 「rotate creature」 button simply spins a rectangular JPEG with a black background — no real 3D — calling it a Potemkin village.
- [pera](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49944366): Notes the example sites contain verbatim copies of FOSS code such as react-dom with no copyright or licensing notice, and is surprised this is tolerated.
- [andrewguenther](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49938981): An OpenAI engineer (disclosed) explains three underrated aspects: per-visitor plugin/connection access, ephemeral throwaway sites, and sites that themselves act as plugins combined with Sign in with ChatGPT, and says Sites won't be abandoned.

## 9. One month coding with GLM 5.3 Flash {#story-9}
*#37 on HN · [wagtail.org](https://wagtail.org/blog/one-month-on-glm-53-flash/) · 214 points · 169 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49934620)*

Wagtail developer ThibWeb tried to spend all of September 2026 using only the open-weight GLM 5.3 Flash (1M context, vision support, available from many providers), tracking usage with AgentsView and energy with the provider Neuralwatt. The experiment was roughly half a failure: only about 1B of 2B tokens went to the target model, because a vibe-coded Wagtail MCP prototype was accidentally run on the non-Flash GLM 5.3 and burned 450M tokens / $150 / 5kWh almost overnight, and provider congestion on popular open models forced switches to DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and Qwen 3.8 Flash. The target model itself stayed within budget ($68, ~4kWh, 365g CO2), and total energy came to ~35kWh instead of a planned 10kWh; takeaways are more local measurement, budgeting for R&D/experiments, multi-agent orchestration roles, and treating cost/energy rather than tokens as the metric.

**Discussion**: The most repeated criticism is that the post is vague and content-free as a model review — several readers (dash-44, raggi, desmondl) say they clicked expecting an assessment of GLM 5.3 Flash and instead got process lessons, with no clear explanation of what was actually 'wrong'; the author replies that the mistake was simply not noticing a single session on the pricier non-Flash GLM 5.3 ate 150% of the monthly budget. A large subthread attacks the energy framing: epistasis argues the $68 vs 4kWh split means electricity is ~1% of inference cost, so the datacenter buildout is a likely overbuild on the scale of the dark-fiber bust, while others counter that local effects (noise, water, grid strain) and training costs are excluded, and the Neuralwatt CTO says energy observability shows a technical-constraint problem today rather than the press's impact narrative. ## 20. Show HN: Made an open-source Lego AI generator {#story-20}
*#49 on HN · [github.com](https://github.com/anteloc/ldraw-nova) · 135 points · 49 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49937916)*

ldraw-nova 是一个开源工具集加 docker 化 Web 应用，让 AI agent 根据一句模型创意生成 LDraw 格式的乐高 CAD 模型，输出 .mpd/.ldr 源文件、3D 查看器、VR（Meta Quest 3）交互、Blender 可编辑的 .glb 以及完整聊天与思考记录。作者的思路是不让 LLM 直接算几何，而是生成 Python 代码来产出 LDraw 指令，再用渲染图做迭代校验，据称由 GPT-6 Astra 与 Claude Opus 5.5 产出可用结果。工具用到了作者自己的 jev-rerank 语义检索（需 TypeSafe API key，否则回退到全文搜索），当前版本 v0.6.0，构建镜像约需 5 GB 磁盘。

**Discussion**: 评论整体非常正面，焦点转向 LLM 生成实物/参数化 CAD 的落地经验：有用户用 FreeCAD + freecad-mcp + Claude 做出了三个一次成功的 3D 打印和铣削零件，并指出模型最难的是理解零件如何装配，作者也认同，并建议向 agent 提供连接件（connector）信息让它们自动检查连接是否正确。关于出错率，作者说给足示例时摆放质量不错，但仍常出现旋转正负号翻转（-90° vs +90°）等问题，靠碰撞检测等校验循环收尾。多位评论者补充了相关工作：Arxiv 论文 2512.15743、CMU 的 BrickGPT（用 LLM 逐块预测并做物理合法性回滚，含 47,000+ 结构数据集）、以及 voxel 转乐高的 brickbuilderai；作者承认 Nova 目前只做碰撞检测、没有物理稳定性判断，做 Technic 机构仍是未完成项，一次 Astra 构建机械结构的花费粗估约 5 美元。也有少数人对「用 AI 玩纯靠想象力的乐高」表达不适，被反驳说不同人有不同的乐趣。

**Worth reading**
- [ash_091](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49938490): 一位用 FreeCAD + freecad-mcp + Claude 的用户分享三个零件首次硬件版本即成功，并指出 AI 最难理解的是零件如何装配到位置。
- [jasonhong](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49940158): CMU 的 BrickGPT 研究者现身：用自回归 LLM 逐块预测加物理感知回滚生成稳定乐高结构，并发布 47,000+ 结构的数据集。
- [antelocnova](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49939082): 作者给出成本量级：Astra 构建一个 Technic 机构大约 5 美元，并解释这正是要做低端 agent 也能用的工具的原因。
