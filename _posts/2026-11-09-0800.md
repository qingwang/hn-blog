## 1. KetchUp – Open-source SketchUp alternative built with Rust and WebGPU {#story-1}
*#1 on HN · [ketchup3d.github.io](https://ketchup3d.github.io/app/) · 426 points · 116 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50418176)*

链接页面现在把该项目标为 BenchUp（标题仍写作 KetchUp）：这是一个免费的 MIT 许可 3D 建模器，面向木工和家具设计，用 Rust 编写，可在桌面浏览器中运行，需要 WebGPU 或 WebGL2 支持，也有 Windows、macOS 和 Linux 原生版本。它支持画线、推拉、精确尺寸输入、组和组件，模型自动保存在本地，可导入 SketchUp .skp 几何体（不含材质和场景），导出 OBJ/STL，没有账号和云存储。它的卖点是「用 SketchUp 的工作流程来规划一张架子、桌子或工作台，然后再动手制作」。

**Discussion**: 讨论大体分成两派。一些人称赞这是对 SketchUp 的用户友好工作流程的继承，因为这个流程变得越来越难以获得：Trimble 砍掉了免费桌面版，取消了永久许可，并把开发重心转向网页版，许多人抱怨每年要支付数百美元订阅费。另一派主要的怀疑意见来自直接试用：画一个矩形就会提示你给对象命名，推拉需要先选取面，默认不吸附到网格，不支持触控板，菜单栏行为也不对——批评者说，它只达到 80% 的完成度，缺少真正让 SketchUp 易用的最后打磨，而且「AI 写不对，大概也修不对」。更广泛的争论是，用 LLM 构建自己的工具是否值得：支持者说，花几个小时就能得到一个可用的工具；反对方说，工具会变成项目本身，只靠一个人维护会陷入大量的边界情况，一个类似 Lightroom 的 vibecoded 克隆做了 5000 次提交后仍然漏洞百出。另外还提到了功能缺口和替代品：SketchUp 的 3D Warehouse 资源库被认为对普通用户是必不可少的价值，FreeCAD、Blender、SolidCraft、hew3d 和 ingetrazo 也被提到是替代品，而且作者已经同意改名，以避免与 KetchUp、SketchUp 发生商标纠纷。

**Worth reading**
- [LinguaBrowse](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50420631): 详细的第一手可用性批评：推拉需要先用箭头工具选中，默认不吸附到网格，每个矩形都要求命名，菜单栏「File」「Edit」「View」的行为也不对——结论是，它没通过成为 SketchUp 所需的打磨测试。
- [sergio](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50420287): 一位 SketchUp 老用户认为，对偶尔做家居改造的人来说，关键的缺失功能是 3D Warehouse——在建模时直接从 SketchUp 里下载现成的电视、家具、椅子模型，比单独拥有一个建模器方便得多。
- [dcoulmbe](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50420114): 作者 dcoulmbe 回应商标担忧，表示会把这个应用改名为 Dovetail3D；另一位评论者随后指出，仓库现在显示的名称是 BenchUp。

## 2. Unlocking any car from X brand in most of the world {#story-2}
*#2 on HN · [shubs.io](https://shubs.io/unlocking-any-car-from-x-brand-in-most-of-the-world/) · 278 points · 92 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50419215)*

这是一篇由 Shubs（infosecau）与 Sam Curry 合著的安全研究文章，作者为保护厂商而以「the automaker」代称，但文中视频与评论基本认定是 Toyota/Lexus。作者反编译安卓 App，顺出一条链条：车牌 → VIN（澳洲各州注册查询站）→ subscriber GUID → 用户在 ForgeRock OpenIDM 身份平台上的邮箱等 PII；又从厂商前端 vendor JS bundle 里找到硬编码的 OpenIDM 管理员凭据。真正的突破在于 WAF 只挡 /openidm/，用 /static%2f..%2fopenidm/ 路径遍历（由 GPT 5.6 Sol Ultra 跑了约六小时找出，作者手动试过 /XUI%2f..%2f 被 403）即可 PATCH 任意用户密码，再配合可跳过多因素验证的 IDTokenROPC 换取移动端 token，从而远程解锁/上锁、鸣笛、启动引擎。

**Discussion**: 评论区的第一条主线是「猜车」：多人从视频里的后视镜、大灯和引擎盖认出第五代 RAV4（XA50），并据「both brands」推断是 Toyota/Lexus；也有人从开源客户端 lexy 的 API 文档反推，还有人拿视频里管理面板配色当线索。第二条主线是威胁模型之争：一派认为这属于需要数周、多人协作的定向攻击，普通窃贼只会拉车门或砸窗，车主不必恐慌；另一派反驳说真正的危害是批量泄露的客户 PII，作者自己也只是顺带提到，而数据泄露正是多数网络犯罪的源头。关于披露，有人质疑文中没写是否上报，随即被指出 Sam Curry 的配套文章（samcurry.net/forgerock）说明两个厂商约两小时内修好并轮换了凭据；也有评论引用该文，指出此前已攻破 Honda、Nissan、Hyundai、Ford、Porsche、Kia、Subaru 等，而这两家此前从未被远程控制过。第三个话题是「联网车本身该不该存在」，从「我 2022 年的车是最后一辆新车」到「远程启动、诊断、事故呼救很有用，问题在控制权而非联网」两派对峙。整体共识大致是：错误很蠢（前端硬编码凭据、生产与非生产共用凭据），但安全本身确实很难。

**Worth reading**

- [jaza](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50421217): 指出厂商其实没自研认证或密码学、大部分做得不错，只违反了两条无需专家也能执行的基本规则——硬编码凭据（尤其在前端代码里）和跨环境共用凭据，而前者本可用静态分析强制检查。
- [avianlyric](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50419676): 称把文中出现的 API 拿去问 LLM，它依据开源第三方客户端 lexy 的文档不到两分钟就判断出是 Toyota/Lexus，并亲自核对无误，觉得这有点吓人。

## 3. I'm not convinced the AI bubble exists {#story-3}
*#4 on HN · [matteoraso.github.io](https://matteoraso.github.io/im-not-convinced-the-ai-bubble-exists.html#im-not-convinced-the-ai-bubble-exists) · 217 points · 300 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50418975)*

A blog post by Matteo Raso arguing that the case for an AI bubble is weaker than the internet assumes. His core claims: the roughly $5 trillion in AI spending is mostly one-off data-centre capex rather than operating cost; Anthropic's S-1 shows $2.08 of revenue per $1 of compute and revenue growing from $41M in 2023 to $16.3B in the first half of 2026; Alphabet, Meta and NVIDIA trade at P/E ratios in the 20s; and a lack of moat doesn't prevent profit, as with the chicken sandwich wars. He also argues AI firms have barely tapped public markets and debt, and dismisses local/open-weight models as something ordinary users will never bother with.

**Discussion**: The strongest pushback is that the article's central premise is wrong: GPUs and networking gear have a 2–5 year useful life, so infrastructure is recurring capex, not a one-off — one commenter recalculates the debt payback over 5–6 years instead of 20 and gets roughly 4x the required revenue. Commenters also attack the $2.08-per-$1-compute figure as cherry-picked (it excludes training and other costs, and Anthropic's losses are reportedly widening), and note 16% yields on CoreWeave/neocloud bonds as the market's own verdict. Several argue the P/E defence is circular because the earnings are themselves funded by debt and equity from AI investors, and that open-weight models plus DeepSeek-style price competition will strip the labs' margins. Defenders of the piece — including a camp arguing bubble talk is motivated by nostalgia and 「SchadenFraud」 — say the utility is undeniable and that bubbles are about timing and overcapitalisation, not whether the tech works; even those concede that a correction with real casualties among model-layer companies is likely while the hyperscalers survive.

**Worth reading**
- [keybored](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50422043): Argues the piece never engages the actual bear case, links Dan Luu's point-by-point rebuttal of Ed Zitron, and notes you can believe AI is useless yet still fear the fallout of a bubble popping.
- [wmf](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50419929): Corrects the 20-year amortisation: most of the cost is GPUs, not buildings, so spreading it over 5–6 years quadruples the revenue needed to service the debt.
- [vannevar](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50420112): Turns the article's own lines against it — 「if you ignore the cost of building cars, they are also very profitable」 — and argues the P/E earnings are circular, derived from debt- and equity-funded AI spending rather than end-user demand.

## 5. Declarative Dragging {#story-5}
*#6 on HN · [declarative-dragging.github.io](https://declarative-dragging.github.io/) · 497 points · 63 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50368481)*

A UIST '26 paper and project page by Joshua Horowitz, Andrew Blinn and Jeffrey Heer describing D2, a model-driven approach where an author specifies which future model states dragging an element should lead to, and the engine speculatively renders those states to view trees, extracts the dragged element's position in each, and synthesizes a smooth drag interaction. Behaviors are composable, with modifiers for floating, snapping and transitions, implemented in a library called Dragology for SVG on the web. Demos include twisted trees (order-preserving maps between trees), a permutation lab with linked parallel representations, and a revived 2022 math game prototype.

**Discussion**: The thread splits between admiration for the demos and a long argument about how hard drag-and-drop really is. A front-page war story about a non-technical PM who could not believe 「just drag-and-drop」 would take a week set off a debate: several commenters (including one who did it in 2010-era browsers and another who built a JS CMS) insist the state machine, ghost element, stacking contexts, touch vs mouse and browser quirks genuinely justify the estimate, while a gamedev commenter argues most apps need only hit-test-on-down and hit-test-on-up and that devs over-engineer beyond business requirements. On the research itself, skeptics ask what is novel given Sketchpad, ThingLab, Juno-2 and Apparatus (which already did speculative rendering), and note D2 requires you to supply the valid transforms rather than deriving them; others worry speculative rendering will blow up with 1000 drop targets or many elements, which a commenter counters by noting lifting one of n items yields only n next states. Andrew Blinn answers as author: transitions are history-independent, which rules out springy/physics-y animations for now, and continuous numeric parameters are handled by a 「vary」 operator. Several people praise the incidental discovery of the Hazel programming environment, and one commenter reports a working HTML prototype built on Solid using the FLIP technique.

**Worth reading**
- [gobdovan](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50418121): Argues the paper omits Sketchpad, ThingLab and Juno-2 from its lineage, credits Apparatus for prior speculative rendering, and notes D2 does not discover valid transforms itself — inverse rendering would have been the more interesting research.
- [edflsafoiewq](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50420990): Reverse-engineers the twisted trees demo into a concrete recipe: write the UI as a function of continuous parameters, compute the cursor's barycentric coordinates between the three nearest drop zones, and interpolate the parameters between their values at those zones.
- [disconcision](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50417304): A commenter says they already have a basic HTML version running on Solid, using the FLIP technique for measurements, though generalizing it across existing frameworks is awkward.

## 6. A Critique of GPUI {#story-6}
*#8 on HN · [blog.zortax.de](https://blog.zortax.de/posts/a-critique-of-gpui) · 77 points · 43 comments · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50420857)*

这是 zortax 博客上一篇长文，把 Rust 原生 GUI 框架的状态模型本身当作问题：它认为 GPUI 和 egui 的状态模型对于复杂桌面应用都是死路，因为一次写操作无法告诉框架「什么变了、谁依赖它」。作者给出的基准测试数据是：3,000 行列表里改一个标签，在每行都带 cached view 的情况下要 8.4 ms，把行写成普通元素则要 92 ms，成本随树规模线性增长（每千行约 3 ms），而手写相等性检查可把「写入相同值」从 7.9 ms 降到 0.9 µs；Longbridge 的 gpui-fast 只能把前者降到 3.4 ms，因为帧仍然要遍历整棵树。文章还比较了 Elm/Iced（一个改动要重建整个 view，39,023 个 widget、7.3 ms）并主张 signals 模型（Floem、Solid、zgui）才是出路——作者同时披露自己在做 zgui，并移植了 GPUI 的 Linux 渲染器到 wgpu。

**Discussion**: 相当一部分评论火力集中在写作本身：多人指认文章是 AI 生成、表格「What a write runs / Who finds the change」难以理解，认为真正思考过这个问题的人应该自己动笔。最实质的反驳是利益冲突指控——有人指出 zgui 上月才发布 0.1.0、只有三个月开发史和唯一一个版本，而 GPUI 的 crates.io 版本也停在一年前的 0.2.2，因此这是披着批评外衣的软文。技术层面的争论集中在即时模式上：一位自称职业做 GUI 工具包的开发者说即时模式「绝不」更容易扩展，每次状态变更都重新求值并布局整个 UI 本质上不可扩展，egui 有时还要多次布局才能收敛；反方则说可以缓存布局，且当大量组件同时更新时 retained mode 的优势会消失，并强调无需在 UI 框架里复制一份状态这一概念上的收益。也有人举 Adobe 类应用（文章评论区提到的 Artcraft 系列，如 vectorcraft、photocraft）作为复杂 UI 成功的例子，随即被反驳其界面本身并不复杂、卡顿投诉多来自后端引擎而非 GUI，还有人说自己跑的 Photocraft 在虚拟机里只有 3 fps。此外有人指出 GPUI 强制手动 notify 是个 footgun，egui 的即时变更模式会造成半个界面显示旧状态、半个显示新状态的撕裂。整体没有形成共识，但「文章论证可信度存疑」的怀疑占了上风。

**Worth reading**
- [x-complexity](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50422111): 指认这是 zgui 的软文：zgui 上月才发布 0.1.0、只有三个月历史和唯一一个版本，并强调框架成熟度是选择移植目标时无法回避的因素。
- [ben-schaaf](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50421976): 一位职业 GUI 工具包开发者直接反驳即时模式更易扩展的说法：每次状态变更都要重新求值并布局整个 UI 本质不可扩展，egui 有时还需多次布局才能达到稳态。
- [slopinthebag](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50422100): 对上述反驳的回应：即时模式也能缓存布局，在大量组件同时更新时 retained mode 的优势会减弱甚至反转，且不必在 UI 框架内复制状态本身就消除了一整类工作。

## 7. Dux: Free, open-source Wayland desktop shell with file manager and theming {#story-7}
